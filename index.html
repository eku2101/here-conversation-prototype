What does this experiment model?

This is an illustrative design experiment, not a prediction or validated measurement of anyone’s feelings. Momentum starts at 70 and recovers by 1 point per present second. Each delivered alert costs 4 points; checking a batch costs 12 points and 4 seconds of attention. Momentum stays between 0 and 100.

Both modes receive six identical updates. One urgent message arrives at 32 seconds. Intentional mode holds the five routine updates until the conversation ends. You can also choose to check held updates yourself. All messages are fictional; the prototype never accesses your phone, sends notifications, or saves personal data.

Try each mode and reflect: when would it feel appropriate to check a phone? Would agreeing on a pause together change the experience?
