How the simulation works

Allowed low-priority alerts cost 15 attention points; contacts and emergencies cost 5. Blocked alerts cost nothing. Opening a message costs another 5 points once. Reconnecting restores 10 points. Mood is Positive at 80–100, Distracted at 50–79, and Disconnected below 50. These are illustrative assumptions, not measured feelings.

WELCOME TO IN-PERSON FOCUS

How is this different from Do Not Disturb?

Apple's Do Not Disturb manages real device notifications. As part of Focus, it can allow exceptions for selected people and apps.

In-Person Focus is a simulation: try fictional notifications and see how your choices affect an illustrative Attention score and Conversation Mood. It explores being present with another person; it does not change your device settings or measure real emotions.
